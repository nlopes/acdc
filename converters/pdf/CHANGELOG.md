### Added

- Initial Typst-backed PDF converter with broad support for AsciiDoc document
  structure, blocks, inlines, navigation, lists, tables, images, source code,
  indexes with `see` and `see-also` relationships, and books. It includes PDF
  themes, page chrome, portrait and landscape layouts, A3/A4/A5 and common US
  page sizes, custom page dimensions and per-document margins, document
  metadata, trusted fonts, safe asset loading, strict
  asset checks, and optional Typst output for diagnostics.
